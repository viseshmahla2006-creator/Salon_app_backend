Meri Devi

Tum naraz ho, aur main jaanta hun —
ye meri harkaton ki wajah se hua.

Bada speech nahi. Bas kuch chhoti si cheezein,
sirf tumhare liye.

— Visesh

Ek diya tumhare liye

Is diye ko chhuo, ise jalao.

Diye pe tap karo 👆
Ye diya isliye — kyunki tumhare bina sab kuch andhera lagta hai. ✨

Ek phool tumhare liye

Ab ye phool tumhare liye.

🌹
Phool pe tap karo 👆
Tum meri zindagi ki sabse khoobsurat cheez ho.
Mere liye tum devi ho, aur main tumse bahut pyaar karta hun. 💖

Dil se ek baat 💌

Meri galti thi, main maanta hun.
Meri harkaton se tumhe dukh pahuncha, uske liye dil se sorry.
Wada karta hun — tumhe hasana mera kaam hai, rulana nahi.
Maaf kar dogi na? 🥺
Theek hai, main intezar karunga.
Par jaane se pehle ye aakhri cheez dekh lo — ye sirf tumhare liye banayi hai.

Tumhare liye… ✨

Bas ek pal…

Ab batao… maaf kar diya na? 🥺❤️
